Voices

Each character gets their own voice. Other people in the story share the “Other men” / “Women” voice with a slightly different pitch each, so you can still tell them apart.

Want nicer voices? Microsoft Edge has very natural “Online (Natural)” voices. On iPhone/Mac, download “Enhanced” voices in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

✨ AI helper

With your own AI key, the app explains each word in its sentence, translates it and gives feedback when you make a mistake. Without a key, meanings come from a free dictionary.

🔒 The key is saved only in this browser on this device. It is never put in your exported progress file. Each request is sent straight from your device to the AI service you chose and uses your account's credit.
